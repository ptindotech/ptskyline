# One accountable group across multiple delivery lines.

Skyline TransOcean Synergi brings together practical operational capability across infrastructure, logistics, energy, technology support and community-facing initiatives. The group is structured to support partners who need a single point of accountability without losing delivery focus.

## Operating model

We start with the immediate need in front of us, match it to the closest capability, and expand coordination where cross-sector delivery is required.

This model keeps communication clear for clients, communities and project partners while maintaining the standards expected for operational work.
